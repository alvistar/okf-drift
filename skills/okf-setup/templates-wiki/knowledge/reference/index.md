# Reference

Stable facts that are needed often and that no task records: identity and tax data, addresses, calendars, the criterion behind a recurring choice. One concept per subject.

Add a category of your own when a kind of concept recurs — for example `contacts/` (one per counterpart), `assets/` (one per thing owned or run), `matters/` (one per open or closed case, with its state dated), `systems/`, `sites/`, `incidents/`. Give it an `index.md` with a short description of what belongs there, and a row in the root index.
